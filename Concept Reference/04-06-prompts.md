## 04-abyss-salvage

Use case ui-mockup. Generate ONE original 16:9 landscape combat game screen concept, a polished playable-looking keyboard-controlled turn-based room roguelike screenshot, NOT poster or concept sheet. Near-orthographic top-down full rectangular 13x7 square grid arena with low boundary walls and a door, entire room visible without occlusion. Player near center-left, four distinctive enemy silhouettes in room, only one nearby melee enemy to right of player has a red ! above head. That one enemy emits a full radius-3 Manhattan diamond of translucent coral red floor tiles with crisp borders including player's tile. No red overlays elsewhere; modest cyan movement tiles on player's other side; red overlays override cyan, actors clearly visible above overlays. Compact top HUD: 3 hearts, MP 2/3, AP 3/3, TURN 04, PLAYER TURN. Bottom inventory 5 slots, 3 filled; bottom right SPACE END TURN; subtle WASD MOVE / ARROWS FIRE / ESC REWIND 2. Single coherent screen, clear hierarchy, beautiful distinctive art, quiet floor and detailed edges. No existing IP logos, no mouse cursor, no perspective occlusion, no long text paragraphs. Theme ABYSS SALVAGE: an old-fashioned deep-sea diver exploring a sunken oceanographic station and shipwreck corridors, recovering mysterious instruments. Player wears round copper diving helmet with warm ivory lamp and dark blue suit, carries compact harpoon launcher. Enemies: marked pale armored crab, angular lanternfish, a jellyfish probe and curled shell creature. Props oxygen cylinders, portholes, corroded pressure door, brass instruments at edges. Loot icons compass, prism, pearl. STYLE bold retro maritime linocut / woodcut print translated into readable 2D game sprites, finely controlled engraved hatching on borders and characters, broad simple silhouette shapes, limited palette of midnight navy, pale seafoam ivory and copper mustard, spot coral red for threats, cyan for movement. Flat printed surfaces with subtle paper grain, NOT pixel art, NOT realistic 3D, NOT watercolor. Ocean darkness atmospheric but all actors and floor readable. HUD brass-instrument framing with elegant concise labels. Title 'ABYSS SALVAGE'.
